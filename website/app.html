Emergency alerts,
even with no app.

Add this site to your home screen and allow emergency alerts once. If someone you accepted as a contact is in danger, your phone rings - straight to the lock screen. On iPhone (iOS 16.4+) these are real notifications, free, with nothing to renew.

  • Sign in with your phone number.
  • Tap Allow emergency alerts once.
  • Alerts ring even when the site is closed - the quiet one stops them the moment someone responds.

Sign in

Your phone number. This is how your contacts find you on Sentri.

Codes arrive in the Sentri Telegram bot. Open it, press Start, then come back and enter your phone number.

Emergency alerts

One tap, then your phone is protected.

On iPhone: use Safari's Share button, choose Add to Home Screen, open Sentri from the new icon, and tap the button there. Apple only sends these alerts to installed sites.

  • You only ever receive alerts for emergencies of people you accepted as a contact in the app.
  • Alerts are receive-only here for now: raising an emergency lives in the app, where the locks, triggers and cameras are.
