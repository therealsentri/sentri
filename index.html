08 / FAQ

The honest
answers.

Who actually gets alerted?

Only the contacts you personally accepted. Nobody else — not strangers, not a monitoring centre, not the internet. You choose the circle before you ever need it.

Does it work if my phone is on silent?

Yes. Sentri emergency alerts bypass silent mode and play at maximum volume. That is the whole point.

Is Sentri only for Nigeria?

No. The app recognises 71 countries with their local police, fire and ambulance numbers, and ships in 19 languages.Different people, different cities, same problem — same button.

How do iPhone users get alerts without an app?

By adding our web app to the home screen from Safari (iOS 16.4+). It delivers the same lock-screen emergency notifications — free for now. Native apps are coming soon to the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

What happens offline?

The local siren works without a connection. If there's no data when you trigger, the alert is saved on the phone and sent automatically the moment you're back online. Delivery to another phone still needs a network — that's how all notifications work.

Can I cancel Premium?

Anytime, from inside the app. ₦1,200/month in Nigeria, $2.99/month internationally. No lock-in.
