Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026. Applies to the Sentri Android app, the Sentri web app on iPhone and Android, and this website.

In one paragraph

Sentri exists for emergencies. We collect the minimum needed to deliver them: your phone number, the emergency contacts you accept, device tokens needed to reach your phone, and — only during an active emergency you or a contact triggered — your live location. We do not sell data, we do not run ads, and we do not read messages, cameras or microphone unless you actively trigger an emergency feature that requires it (e.g., the heart-rate camera while you are using it).

What we collect

  • Account: phone number, sign-in device identifiers.
  • Contacts: the Sentri users you accept as emergency contacts — no other roster is accessed.
  • Push tokens: one per device, so Sentri can deliver emergency notifications to your lock screen.
  • Emergencies: alert text, timestamps, and live location received only by your accepted contacts, only while the emergency is active (free users: live-only; Premium: up to 1 year of your own share history).
  • Payments (Premium): handled by Paystack (Nigeria) or Creem (international) as our payment processors. Sentri never sees or stores your card number.

What we never do

  • No selling, renting, or advertising use of your data.
  • No contact roster scans, SMS reading, or background microphone access.
  • No location tracking outside active emergency shares you started.

Security

Push subscriptions are encrypted at rest, access tokens are short-lived, and payments are signature-verified server-side. If a security concern affects you, report it to therealsentri@gmail.com.

Your choices

  • Stop emergency alerts at any time (web app: one tap; Android: notification settings).
  • Delete your account and its data by emailing therealsentri@gmail.com.
  • Cancel your Premium subscription in-app at any time.

Support: therealsentri@gmail.com
